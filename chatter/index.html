Thoughts on AI in Sports

Sports and AI might seem like an odd pair at first, but they fit together surprisingly well. Take sailing: reading the wind, the water, and the sail shape is a skill built over years. But a lot of that perception can be measured — and that's exactly where computer vision comes in. When I started my project on intelligent sail-shaping assistance, I thought the hard part would be the algorithms. In reality, the hard part is understanding the domain deeply enough to ask the right questions. AI won't replace the feel of the sport. But it can be an incredible training companion — giving instant, objective feedback that used to take an expert's eye. That's the direction I want to keep exploring: not replacing human skill, but amplifying it.
