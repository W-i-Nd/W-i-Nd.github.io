## A Study on a Computer Vision-Based Intelligent Sail-Shaping Control Assistance System for Sailing Training

Sailing is a sport of precision. The shape of the sail directly affects speed and control — but for trainees, reading the sail and adjusting it correctly takes years of practice. My project asks: **can computer vision help?**

### The problem

- Sail shape is hard to observe and judge, especially for beginners
- Feedback from coaches is subjective and not always available
- Existing systems are either too expensive or too complex for routine training

### The idea

Use cameras to capture the sail in real time, analyze its shape with computer vision algorithms, and provide **intelligent assistance** for sail adjustment during training.

### What I've learned along the way

- Camera calibration and image preprocessing
- Contour detection for sail shape extraction
- Building a feedback pipeline that turns visual data into actionable advice
- Combining **Python** (prototyping & AI) with **C++** (performance-critical parts)

### Why it matters

This kind of assistive system could make sailing training more accessible and effective — helping beginners learn faster and letting coaches focus on strategy rather than basic corrections.

This is still an ongoing study, and I'm excited to keep developing it. Stay tuned for updates!
